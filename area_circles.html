Area circles

Type numbers, get a circle cut into polygons whose areas are proportional to them. Each cell's area is solved to within a fraction of a percent, not eyeballed.

One per line. 5 · Rent, 60 · Brazil, 204, Americas. A line of plain numbers like 5 3 2 8 1 works too.

The layout is a weighted (power) Voronoi treemap, after Nocaj & Brandes, Computing Voronoi Treemaps (2012): build the power diagram, slide each cell's site to its centroid, then Newton-step the weights until every area matches its target. Area is the quantity — colour and the printed number say the same thing again, so nothing rests on colour alone.

Ported from the Python version in ex_ml (voronoi_treemap.py, circle_area_chart.py), which produces the same layout offline.
